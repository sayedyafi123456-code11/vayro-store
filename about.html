VAYRO is an independent streetwear brand founded by Yafi in India. It begins with a simple point of view: everyday clothing can carry a clear identity through considered design, confident silhouettes and attention to detail.

INDEPENDENT BY VISION · BUILT WITH INTENT
OUR POINT OF VIEW

LESS NOISE. MORE IDENTITY.

VAYRO explores the space between everyday essentials and expressive design. The visual direction is modern, minimal and confident, with a focus on form, function and a distinctive brand language. Each idea is developed with intention rather than following every passing trend.

THE FIRST DESIGN CONCEPT

CORE 001 — THE SIGNATURE TEE.

CORE 001 introduces the VAYRO design language through a proposed black oversized boxy T-shirt concept, a minimal silver V mark and original graphic direction. It is a design preview, not a product currently available to purchase. Fabric, fit, artwork and production details still need to be sample-tested and approved.
